**File format** (`main.json` and each recheck): `{"holdings": [{"sym", "status" (Hold/Add/Trim/Full exit), "notes" (the reason in one line; a Full exit says urgent, with its dated event, or not urgent)}], "allocation": [{"sym", "name", "action" (Buy/Add/Hold/Trim/Exit), "target_weight" (a fraction, 0.10 = 10%), "target_value" (₹), "buy_below" (₹), "trim_above" (the position's trim line, a fraction of the portfolio), "top_up" (null, or {"waiting_for", "next_check"} for a position below its target that waits on a marker or a price), "notes"}], "caps": {"largest_stock", "largest_sector": {"sector", "share"}, "emerging", "bent_rule", "government", "liquid"} (target shares, as fractions), "reasons": [...]}` (allocation rows in the order they should appear on the tab).
