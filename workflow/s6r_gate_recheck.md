# S6R.n: gate recheck checklist (deep tier, run per `/workflow/recheck.md`)

Work file: `<run>/gate/bNN.json` (recheck 1) or `bNN_rc1.json`; your output: `bNN_rc<N>.json` as {"entries": [the corrected list, same format], plus the fields `/workflow/recheck.md` adds}. Read `/workflow/s6_gate.md` and `/principles/filter_rules.md` first, and each company's `<run>/gate/<SYM>.json`.

A wrong REJECT is never seen again, while every NEXT gets the full analysis and two rechecks in S11. So the weight of this recheck is on rejects:
- **Every REJECT** that isn't clearly dead on return math, **every borderline case** (best CAGR within ~5 points of the line) and **every floor call**: the full checklist below.
- **Clear REJECTs on return math:** check only that the math and its inputs are right.
- **Every WATCH:** a price watch: check that it fails on price alone (no floor failed, growth evidence intact; otherwise REJECT) and recompute its buy-below price on the diluted EPS; every WATCH records its `watch_kind`. A growth watch: check that the drag is named and dated in the filings, that no floor fails, and recompute the CAGR without the drag.
- **Every REJECT short on growth alone** in the focus set: check whether the growth watch applies.
- **Every NEXT:** check only the floors and integrity items (3 below), that nothing in the filings was missed (5), and the sector (6). A NEXT whose g fails the gate step's capacity test, with no floor failed, stays NEXT with the capacity-led figure recorded for S11 (in `contrary_search` and the entry's `why`), not in `unresolved`; a floor failure decides REJECT.

1. **Inputs:** where cash flows were regrouped (often SME filers), compare the 3-year CFO across both annual reports' columns. P/E basis (one-offs normalised), growth used and exit P/E, each as the gate standard sets it: against the cached screener data, the gate file and the filing.
2. **Math:** redo the multiple and CAGR for the projection length used, from the normalised P/E the entry states and on the diluted share count (where the exit P/E equals today's, est_cagr equals g), and check whether another length would clear the line.
3. **Floors:** each floor of the gate standard, and promoter buying against the promoter selling and buying rule's tests; the light promoter track record check was done and settled (clean, mixed or poor, even when another floor already rejects, since it changes what a re-gate needs), and a poor record decided REJECT; each REJECT's `verified_against` points to the right page.
4. **Contrary evidence** for each REJECT checked: anything in the guidance documents, filings or figures that shows the growth or exit P/E was too low, or that the failed floor actually passes (a temporary cause, an improving trend, the trajectory rule; for a debt floor failing narrowly while falling, build the ratio quarter by quarter from the company's net-debt walk and apply the trajectory clause explicitly).
5. **Missed evidence:** a newer results filing, a guidance change, or a governance item the gate didn't read.
6. **Sector:** the confirmed sector follows from what the company does (a correction goes in the entry's `sector` field in your output, with the reason in `recheck_log`; `gate/<SYM>.json` stays as the gate wrote it); no reject leans on the company being outside a tailwind sector, and no emerging company is rejected on trailing return math, and a participant is gated on its main business's sector.

**For the orchestrator:** after a batch's last recheck, run `python scripts/gate_merge.py` (writes `<run>/gate/decisions.csv` from each batch's latest file). If a recheck finds an integrity flag, a deep-tier subagent reads the filing before the reject stands. To re-gate a company the gate standard lets back in, add it to `<run>/regate.txt` and re-run `triage.py`. After the last batch, `python -I scripts/sectors.py order --from-gate` applies the gate's sector corrections to `sector_of.tsv`.
