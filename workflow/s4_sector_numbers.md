# S4: sector numbers (scripts, run by the orchestrator; full mode, and stage numbers only in recheck mode)

Everything here is counting and calculating, so it is scripts only; S5's subagent reads the results. Every rule and threshold is in the `--help` of `scripts/sectors.py` (each mode's own `--help` too). If screener.in can't be reached for a fetch here: `/sources/screener.md`.

1. **Industry labels and fund holdings** for every company S3 queued and every holding: `python -I scripts/sectors.py syms --out <run>/label_fetch.txt --history <run>/history_fetch.csv` (queued companies and holdings without a saved `scr/overview.json`, or with a `scr/shareholding.json` older than the run quarter; and universe companies with no price history), then `uvx --from screener-mcp==0.5.0 python scripts/scr_company.py --syms-file <run>/label_fetch.txt --only overview,shareholding` (without `--skip-saved`, which would skip the stale shareholding the list asks for) and `uvx --from screener-mcp==0.5.0 python scripts/mb_history.py --csv <run>/history_fetch.csv`, one after the other in the background (public, no login; never beside another screener fetch). What the label is worth: `/principles/sector_rules.md`.
2. **Clusters:** `python -I scripts/sectors.py clusters` → `<run>/sector_clusters.csv`: per industry label, the universe count, the queued count, the queued share against the universe share (over-representation), its z-score, and the holdings in it. Companies without a label are counted under `(no label)` and listed.
3. **Discovery-stage numbers:** `python -I scripts/sectors.py stage` → `<run>/sector_stage.csv`: per industry label (and per sector once `sectors.json` exists: `--sectors <run>/sectors.json`), the median P/E now against its own 10-year history (percentile), the median 1-year price return against the whole universe's, the median change in institutional holding (FIIs + DIIs) over 4 quarters with its coverage, the count of listings in the last 24 months and of QIPs and preferential issues in the last 12 months (from saved announcements, with coverage), and a suggested stage (undiscovered / early / recognised / crowded) by the script's fixed rule. S5 sets the stage and gives a reason wherever it differs from the suggestion. What the stage means and its limits: `/principles/sector_rules.md`.
4. **Recheck mode:** no new universe, so current prices and P/Es come from each sector member's `scr/overview.json`: `python -I scripts/sectors.py syms --stage-only --sectors <run>/sectors.json --out <run>/label_fetch.txt` (members whose overview is missing or older than 14 days), the `scr_company.py --only overview` fetch above on that list, then step 3 with `--stage-only --sectors <run>/sectors.json` (the copied map; members come from the last full run's `universe.csv`), so S5's review sees each sector's stage drift since its call.

Done when every queued company and holding has a label or a recorded gap (the fetch's failures listed in `progress.md`), and every industry with 5 or more universe companies has its stage numbers. Record the counts in `progress.md`.
