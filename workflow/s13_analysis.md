# S13.0: deep analysis (deepest tier, one company per subagent; both modes)

You analyse one company in depth and write one analysis file (`main`); two rechecks then correct it (`/workflow/recheck.md`). Never read an earlier analysis of this company unless your brief says this is a re-research, and then only after your own analysis is finished, to write the "what changed" note. Do not edit `/principles/investing_rules.md`, the sheet or any file outside your output. Working directory: the project root (PowerShell). Your brief gives the company, the run folder (`runs/<FYyyQn>/`), the mode, whether the company is a holding, whether this is a re-research, and in recheck mode the S11 result that sent it (`<run>/marker_check.csv` rows).

## Before analysing: newer results?

If the company has declared results newer than `latest_declared_quarter` in `index.json`, stop and reply only `NEW_RESULTS: <FYyyQn>` (unless your brief says the loop cap is reached; then note it and carry on).

## The analysis
Apply `/principles/investing_rules.md` throughout, weighing what the company passes against what it fails. Your results must not depend on today's share price: give EPS paths, exit P/Es and judgements only; the sheet computes P/E, PEG, multiples and CAGR from live prices.
1. **Figures:** resolve every `facts_check.json` mismatch against the filing (rule out definition gaps first); list each genuine source-vs-filing mismatch (screener or another provider) as a data flag. Fetch anything missing yourself (per `/principles/data_rules.md`), say what you fetched, and add a line to `/workflow/fetch_learnings.md` (re-read it just before writing; no duplicates) so S12 fetches it next time.
2. **Normalised trailing EPS:** strip one-offs (the earnings evidence rule).
3. **Non-negotiables** (the non-negotiables): Pass / Bent / Fail for each of the four, with the evidence and its source; name any that fail.
4. **Every negative** (the trajectory rule): the cause with its source, temporary or structural, management's record on it, whether it is improving now. Keep a guidance-vs-delivery scorecard for the last 4-8 quarters.
5. **Sector case:** is this company capturing the sector's tailwind (its own orders, capacity, share, margins against the sector's profit-capture test), or riding the label without it? Which of the playbook's capture signals and end signals it shows. A company outside a tailwind sector is judged on its own drivers the same way.
6. **Horizon and stage** per the visibility rule and the horizon rules in `/principles/investing_rules.md`: the one thesis horizon, and the discovery stage (the sector's from `sectors.json`, or the company's own with a reason: e.g. an under-owned name in a crowded sector). If the company is a holding for which no horizon's test holds, give the closest horizon and set `horizon_holds` to false.
7. **EPS paths:** base EPS at year 1; base and bear EPS at the horizon's end (year 2, 5 or 7); paths to shorter horizons' ends may be added for reference. Build them from guidance (haircut by the scorecard), order book, capacity coming online and margins; say how.
8. **Exit P/E for each horizon you give paths for**, set exactly as the valuation and return math rule says, including both sanity checks. The own 5-year median P/E: the "Historical PE 5Years" figure in `<run>/universe.csv`, or computed from year-end P/Es in screener's ratios table (`scr/ratios.json`) (say which). Name the hand-picked peers with their 5-year median P/Es, found the same way. Say what the re-rating case rests on (stage, the sector's discovery, the company's own P/E history).
9. **Evidence quality** (High / Medium / Low), graded per `/principles/data_rules.md`.
10. **Thesis markers** meeting the exit rules and the thesis-marker standard in `/principles/sector_rules.md`. Each {id, level ("company"/"sector"), text, metric, op, threshold (a number, or [lo, hi] with op "between"; % metrics in percent), event (for metric "event"), due (YYYY-MM-DD), by (true when the line is to be reached by the due date; absent or false when it must hold every quarter until then), check ("script" when `thesis.py check` supports the metric, else "manual"), source, sector_marker_id (sector level)}; the supported metrics and how each is computed are in `scripts/thesis.py check --help`, and `thesis.py register` refuses markers that break these rules.
11. **Bent rules** (the bent-rule tier), **thesis**, **kill condition** and **review date**.
12. **Sector notes:** if this company shows a sector note in `/principles/investing_rules.md` is wrong or missing, propose the change (old and new wording, reason, evidence). Don't edit the rules file.
