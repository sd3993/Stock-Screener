# S11.0: marker check (script, one company; recheck mode)

After S10 has refreshed the company's screener data and documents: `python -I scripts/thesis.py check <SYM>` (metrics, states and rules in its `--help`). Run `python -I scripts/thesis.py check --sectors` once per run before S5's review, so the sector markers' script states are in `<run>/marker_check.csv` too.

What it does: reads the company's thesis (`runs/companies/<SYM>/thesis.json`: horizon, stage, results used, basis, and its 4-6 markers, company-level and sector-level), the sector markers' states (`<run>/sectors.json`), and the saved data (`data/companies/<SYM>/scr/` statements on the thesis's basis, shareholding, `shp_history`, announcements, pledge, corporate actions; a figure S12 read from a filing into `facts.json` `marker_inputs` takes precedence). For each "script" marker it computes the metric for the newest declared quarter and compares it with the line; a marker to be reached "by" its due date stays pending until then; "manual" markers due by now are listed for the orchestrator. It writes one row per marker to `<run>/marker_check.csv` (sym, marker id, level, metric, value, line, due, state: held / near / broken / pending / due-manual / no-data, source, note) and one row per company with its **action**, and appends the same rows to the company's marker-check history (`runs/companies/<SYM>/marker_checks.csv`). The action is the first that applies:
1. **integrity review:** an announcement since the thesis date whose title matches an integrity term (auditor resignation, forensic audit, SEBI/ED order, fraud, default, pledge invocation, insolvency; the list is in the script). Integrity flags override every marker (the integrity flags rule).
2. **re-analyse:** a marker broken (company-level, or its sector's), no thesis file (a new holding or an old analysis without markers), or the half-yearly re-analysis due (two newer quarters declared since the thesis's results used).
3. **verify:** a marker within 10% of its line (2 points for % metrics), a manual marker due, or a metric the script couldn't compute (`no-data`, never passed silently: e.g. EPS across a split, growth off a loss-making base, a missing statement).
4. **hold:** every marker held or pending.

**Orchestrator:** act per the recheck-mode order of work in `/workflow/orchestrator.md`. Manual markers due go with the company to S12 (verify), which reads them from their named source into `facts.json` `marker_inputs`. A `verify` company goes back through S11 after S12 has rebuilt `facts.json`, and the second result stands. How a broken marker weighs against the position (hold, trim, exit) is decided in S15 under `/principles/investing_rules.md`, not here.
