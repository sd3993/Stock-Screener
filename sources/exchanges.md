# BSE/NSE directly (for what the screener scripts (`/sources/screener.md`) don't cover; `/principles/data_rules.md`)
The screener-mcp library's NSE client (used by `scr_company.py`) sends browser-like headers and loads NSE's homepage for cookies; NSE's terms of use ban automated collection. The user chose to keep using the libraries as they are (4 Nov 2026); don't add header spoofing of our own. No exchange offers a free API; licensed feeds cost lakhs a year (NSE corporate data, BSE data products).
- **BSE PDFs:** `www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/<id>.pdf` (same-day filings often under `AttachLive`) with `curl.exe` and its default identity; `AnnPdfOpen.aspx?Pname=<id>.pdf` is the same file. `bse_pdf.py SYM "regex" files...` downloads and extracts. Some transcript links return an HTML page from both paths: use the NSE copy. Large companies sometimes file only a cover letter linking to a website transcript: read the linked file and say so.
- **BSE APIs** (bot-protected, so scripts can't call them): `bse_fetch.py` and `bse_fetch.js` cover the usual items for a batch of about 20 companies, saved to `data/companies/<SYM>/bse/`. For anything else, call api.bseindia.com with fetch() from a www.bseindia.com tab (CORS allowed): `Integratedfinancedata/w?scripcode=` (iXBRL filings since the Mar-2025 quarter), `ConsolidatePledge/w`, `TabResults_SHP/w` (last 3 quarters), `shpSecSummery_New/w?qtrid=<n>.00&scripcode=` (one quarter's pattern; Sep 2026 = 131), `CorporateAction/w`, `ListofScripData/w` (all scrips: `SCRIP_CD`, `scrip_id` = NSE-style symbol), announcements `AnnSubCategoryGetData/w?pageno=&strCat=-1&strPrevDate=YYYYMMDD&strScrip=<code>&strSearch=P&strToDate=&strType=C&subcategory=-1` (12-month window).
- **Old data (checked 4 Nov 2026):** shareholding pattern of any quarter from Mar 2001 (qtrid = 29 + quarters since Mar 2001; Mar 2008 = 57) downloads with plain curl as HTML from `www.bseindia.com/corporates/ShareholdingPattern.aspx?scripcd=<code>&flag_qtr=1&qtrid=<n>.00&Flag=New` (omit `Flag=New` before Jun 2006): promoter shares and %, institutions, pledge from 2009, delisted companies too. `bse_shp.py` fetches and parses them (`--rows FILE` of sym and as_of/start, default the playbook base data's `sample.csv`; `--want SYM[:CODE]=YYYY-MM..YYYY-MM`; `--plan`; `--reparse`): raw pages and `history.json` in `data/companies/<SYM>/bse/shp/`, log in the run folder; `mb_features.py` reads it. About 1.95 s a request at the default 1.5 s gap, about 3.5 s at `--gap 3`. Settled (4 Nov 2026): the page has data through Jun 2022 at most (some companies stop at Dec 2021), blank after and before listing; from Dec 2015 it shows the new-format filing mapped onto the old table (FPIs as "Qualified Foreign Investor", employee-trust shares left out of the total, so promoter % can differ from NSE's by up to ~0.03); its pledge column is % of the category's own shares; promoter % matched NSE's for Sep 2021 - Jun 2022 (Grindwell, Suzlon exactly; Infosys within 0.03). Browser-only: `AnnualReport_New/w?scripcode=` (every annual report from ~1997 with PDF links; the PDFs, `HIS_ANN_RPT/HISTANNR/<yr>/<BSE name of the time>-<code>-MARCH-<yr>.PDF` or `bseplus/AnnualReport/<code>/<code>03<yy>.pdf` from 2010, curl fine and have a text layer), `SHPQNewFormat/w?scripcode=` (quarter list), `Corp_detailedResult_Transpose_ng/w?Scrip_cd=&Qtr=<qtrid>.00` (structured quarterly results from the Sep-2007 quarter: sales, expenses, other income, interest, depreciation, PBT, tax, PAT, equity, EPS; banks in their own format). Annual-report links by pattern (checked 4 Nov 2026, `ar_statements.py`): `HIS_ANN_RPT/HISTANNR/<y>/...-MARCH-<y>.PDF` holds the fiscal year ending in `<y>` (HMT 2004 = Mar 2004, ESAB India 2006 = Dec 2006); its name part is BSE's name of the time, often not today's (no variant of Balkrishna Industries' name hits), so misses go to the browser's `AnnualReport_New` list (a browser subagent, 4 Nov 2026: from any bseindia.com tab, one un-awaited page-script loop of plain `fetch()` calls to `AnnualReport_New/w?scripcode=<code>`, 3 s apart, polled for its result, did 43 companies in ~20 tool calls with no refusals; its year label matches the PDF path year; it writes `ar_links.csv`, and `ar_statements.py fetch` checks each report's year from its headings); a wrong guess returns 200 with an HTML page, so check for `%PDF`. Screener's annual-report "year" is sometimes the filing year (Aditya Birla Nuvo "2008" = FY09): the year comes from the statement headings. Some listed PDFs are scans with no text layer (Ratnamani FY13-15); no OCR tool is installed, so a subagent reads those pages as images. NSE's SHP list starts Sep 2021 whatever the dates; its annual-report list from ~2010. Screener's Excel export has 10 years (fewer than the page's 12) and its yearly shareholding tab starts Mar 2017.
- **NSE PDFs:** curl to nsearchives.nseindia.com hangs; the same paths download from `https://archives.nseindia.com/corporate/...` (`curl.exe -s -m 60 -o <file> <url>`). Fallback: fetch as base64 inside an nsearchives tab and decode with `save_b64_pdf.py`.
- **NSE APIs** (scripts reach them through the screener-mcp library's NSE session, as `scr_company.py` does; otherwise from a www.nseindia.com tab): `/api/integrated-filing-results` (also `index=sme`), `/api/corporate-share-holdings-master`, `/api/corporates-pit?index=equities|sme&symbol=&from_date=&to_date=` (insider trades, pledges), `/api/corporate-announcements?index=equities&symbol=&from_date=&to_date=`. `corporate-announcements` silently drops rows over wide windows: query one month at a time.
- **Exchange libraries (reviewed 4 Nov 2026, not adopted):** `nsepython` 2.101 (aeron7, GPL-3, single file `rahu.py`; needs curl_cffi, pandas, scipy) reaches nseindia.com only by impersonating Chrome's TLS fingerprint (curl_cffi) to get past Akamai Bot Manager, plus an optional Playwright helper that passes Akamai's JS challenge; `bse` 3.3.4 (BennyThadikaran/BseIndiaApi, GPL-3; needs requests, mthrottle; throttles itself to 8 requests/s) reaches api.bseindia.com, which answers plain curl with Akamai "Access Denied", by sending a Chrome User-Agent, Origin and Referer. Both are bot-detection workarounds, so agents don't run them; no eval, shell-out or telemetry found in either. `bse` has no annual-report, shareholding or detailed-results function anyway (announcements, corporate actions, result calendar, `TabResults_PAR` snapshot of 3 periods, quotes, bhavcopy, scrip lookup). Other free libraries (jugaad-data, nselib, nsetools, bsedata) rely on the same browser imitation. `indian-market-mcp` (afthabvp, MIT, commit 6a34165, reviewed 4 Nov 2026) is the same: its NSE client impersonates Chrome's TLS fingerprint (curl_cffi) and primes cookies; it has no BSE endpoint (its "BSE" is yfinance `.BO`), and its NSE feeds are current data only (no annual-report lists, no pre-2010 history); the rest is yfinance, AMFI, mfapi, metals.dev and Google News RSS. BSE's legacy pages (`corporates/results?...`, `HistoricalAnnualreport?...`) now return only the Angular shell that calls the API, so old structured results and old annual-report lists stay browser-only.
- **NSE official MCP servers (no account, plain requests work, checked 4 Nov 2026):** `https://mcp.nseindia.in/bhavcopy/cm/mcp` (21 tools: `get_stock_history` daily OHLCV ≤3 months a call, `get_ltp_by_date`, `get_corporate_actions` with `fromDate`/`toDate` as YYYY-MM-DD, index valuation/history, symbol search) and `.../cmmkt/mcp` (live quotes, gainers/losers). JSON-RPC over HTTP: `initialize` returns an `Mcp-Session-Id`, but the nodes behind the load balancer don't share sessions, so a call 404s about half the time: retry the same call up to 5 times, ~1.6 s apart. Prices only from about Nov 2021 (INFY 2016 and 2008 come back null; GRINDWELL 2022-05-27 close 1743.30 vs screener's weekly 1743.05), some NSE symbols missing (HAWKINCOOK not found), corporate actions from 2007 (same rows as `scr/corporate_actions`). Adds little beyond what we have.
- **BSE code for an NSE symbol (plain curl, checked 4 Nov 2026):** join NSE's `https://archives.nseindia.com/content/equities/EQUITY_L.csv` (SYMBOL, ISIN) with the day's BSE bhavcopy `https://www.bseindia.com/download/BhavCopy/Equity/BhavCopy_BSE_CM_0_0_0_<YYYYMMDD>_F_0000.CSV` (`FinInstrmId` = scrip code, `ISIN`, `TckrSymb`) on ISIN: 2,449 of 2,627 NSE symbols matched (COLPAL 500830, CRISIL 500092, EXIDEIND 500086, COROMANDEL 506395, all correct). Delisted companies: old bhavcopies `.../BhavCopy/Equity/EQ<DDMMYY>_CSV.ZIP` (e.g. 300610) also download by curl; they carry code and name (no ISIN in that old format).
- **Promoter sales:** when the insider (PIT) feed and the SAST Reg 29(2) disclosure disagree on size, read the Reg 29(2) per-person annexure first (it covers everyone acting in concert). Settle promoter-group membership from Reg 31(4) declarations and the shareholding pattern's promoter list. A fall in promoter share count with unchanged total shares means a sale, transfer out or reclassification.
- **Scanned PDFs** give no text (no OCR here); render pages as images and read them, and say so. Extract page images with pypdf's `page.images` (Pillow); image-mask pages that won't render are read from the OCR text layer and checked by arithmetic. Mixed-raster SAST PDFs: wrap each layer's raw stream (pypdf) in a TIFF header, convert to PNG (.NET System.Drawing) and read the image.
- **Browser:** plain web fetchers can't open nseindia.com or bseindia.com; don't spoof a browser. Only one browser subagent at a time works on nseindia.com or bseindia.com (both rate-limit and bot-check, and parallel sessions share that limit); it uses one tab of its own and closes it when done. Long page scripts: start them un-awaited and poll; save large results with `save_js_file.py`. Never wait on a script or download more than about a minute: reload and retry up to three times, then report the company as unfinished.
- **XBRL quirks:** owners'-share PAT tags are sometimes 0 or wrong for a single quarter (cross-check the results PDF); some filers tag income tax paid under investing.
- **BSE's API is bot-protected:** scripts and curl get 403, and working around it (browser headers, cookies or fingerprints faked) isn't allowed. BSE's API is browser-only; its PDFs download with plain curl. NSE goes first for NSE-listed companies.
- **Browser (bseindia.com, nseindia.com):** bseindia.com's CSP blocks `eval`, so a snippet can't be loaded from a local server; the browser can't reach local addresses either. How an agent drives the browser and gets data out of it is in that agent's own instruction files.
- **BSE announcements API** (`AnnSubCategoryGetData`) sends 50 rows a page and no page count: page until a page adds no new `NEWSID`.
