# S4: sector numbers (scripts, run by the orchestrator; full mode, and stage numbers only in recheck mode)

Everything here is counting and calculating, so it is scripts only; S5's helper reads the results. Script details and tests: `runs/new_scripts_spec.md` (`sector_numbers.py`, to write).

1. **Industry labels and fund holdings** for every company S3 queued and every holding: `python -I tools/scripts/sector_numbers.py syms --out <run>/label_fetch.txt` (queued companies and holdings without a saved `scr/overview.json`, or with a `scr/shareholding.json` older than the run quarter), then `uvx --from screener-mcp==0.5.0 python tools/scripts/scr_company.py --syms-file <run>/label_fetch.txt --only overview,shareholding --skip-saved` in the background (public, no login; never beside another screener fetch; a half-hourly watchdog, `COMMON_AI.md`). The label is screener's copy of NSE's industry classification: a coarse hint that S5 and the gate correct from what each company actually does.
2. **Clusters:** `python -I tools/scripts/sector_numbers.py clusters` → `<run>/sector_clusters.csv`: per industry label, the universe count, the queued count, the queued share against the universe share (over-representation), its z-score, and the holdings in it. Companies without a label are counted under `(no label)` and listed.
3. **Discovery-stage numbers:** `python -I tools/scripts/sector_numbers.py stage` → `<run>/sector_stage.csv`: per industry label (and per sector once `sectors.json` exists: `--sectors <run>/sectors.json`), the median P/E now against its own 10-year history (percentile), the median 1-year price return against the whole universe's, the median change in institutional holding (FIIs + DIIs) over 4 quarters with its coverage, the count of listings in the last 24 months and of QIPs and preferential issues in the last 12 months (from saved announcements, with coverage), and a suggested stage (undiscovered / early / recognised / crowded) by the fixed rule in the spec. S5 sets the stage and gives a reason wherever it differs from the suggestion.
4. **Recheck mode:** step 3 only, with `--stage-only --sectors <run>/sectors.json` (the copied map), so S5's review sees each sector's stage drift since its call.

Done when every queued company and holding has a label or a recorded gap (the fetch's failures listed in `progress.md`), and every industry with 5 or more universe companies has its stage numbers. Record the counts in `progress.md`.
