You analyse one company in depth and write one analysis file (`main`); two rechecks then correct it (`process/recheck.md`). Never read an earlier analysis of this company unless your brief says this is a re-research, and then only after your own analysis is finished, to write the "what changed" note. Do not edit `process/investing_rules.md`, the sheet or any file outside your output. Working directory: the project root (PowerShell). Your brief gives the company, the run folder (`runs/<FYyyQn>/`), the mode, whether the company is a holding, whether this is a re-research, and in recheck mode the S11 result that sent it (`<run>/marker_check.csv` rows).

## The analysis
Apply `process/investing_rules.md` throughout, weighing what the company passes against what it fails. Your results must not depend on today's share price: give EPS paths, exit P/Es and judgements only; the sheet computes P/E, PEG, multiples and CAGR from live prices.
1. **Figures:** resolve every `facts_check.json` mismatch against the filing (rule out definition gaps first); list each genuine source-vs-filing mismatch (screener or another provider) as a data flag. Fetch anything missing yourself (per `process/data_rules.md`), say what you fetched, and add a line to `process/fetch_learnings.md` (re-read it just before writing; no duplicates) so S12 fetches it next time.
2. **Normalised trailing EPS:** strip one-offs (rule 6).
3. **Non-negotiables** (rule 1): Pass / Bent / Fail for each of the four, with the evidence and its source; name any that fail.
4. **Every negative** (rule 2): the cause with its source, temporary or structural, management's record on it, whether it is improving now. Keep a guidance-vs-delivery scorecard for the last 4-8 quarters.
5. **Sector case:** is this company capturing the sector's tailwind (its own orders, capacity, share, margins against the sector's profit-capture test), or riding the label without it? Which of the playbook's capture signals and end signals it shows. A company outside a tailwind sector is judged on its own drivers the same way.
6. **Horizon and stage** per rule 7 and the horizons section of the rules file: the one thesis horizon (short ~1-2 years, medium ~3-5, long 5-7+) set by the tailwind's remaining duration and the company's own visibility, and the discovery stage (the sector's from `sectors.json`, or the company's own with a reason: e.g. an under-owned name in a crowded sector). If the company is a holding for which no horizon's test holds, give the closest horizon and set `horizon_holds` to false.
7. **EPS paths:** base EPS at year 1; base and bear EPS at the horizon's end (year 2, 5 or 7); paths to shorter horizons' ends may be added for reference. Build them from guidance (haircut by the scorecard), order book, capacity coming online and margins; say how.
8. **Exit P/E for each horizon you give paths for**, set exactly as rule 8 says, including both sanity checks. The own 5-year median P/E: the "Historical PE 5Years" figure in `<run>/universe.csv`, or computed from year-end P/Es in screener's ratios table (`scr/ratios.json`) (say which). Name the hand-picked peers with their 5-year median P/Es, found the same way. Say what the re-rating case rests on (stage, the sector's discovery, the company's own P/E history).
9. **Evidence quality** (High / Medium / Low): filed orders and running capacity rank above guidance, guidance above extrapolation.
10. **Thesis markers:** 4-6 dated markers that would show the thesis holding or breaking: at least one sector-level (copy the id of the sector marker it rests on) and at least three company-level (e.g. quarterly revenue or profit growth against a floor, margin band, order inflow, capacity commissioning by a date, cash conversion, promoter holding or pledge). Each {id, level ("company"/"sector"), text, metric, op, threshold, event, due, check ("script" when `marker_check.py` supports the metric, else "manual"), source}; metric names `marker_check.py` supports are listed in `runs/new_scripts_spec.md`. Thresholds are set where a miss would genuinely change the case, not at the first wobble.
11. **Bent rules** (rule 15), **thesis**, **kill condition** and **review date**.
12. **Sector notes:** if this company shows a sector note in `process/investing_rules.md` is wrong or missing, propose the change (old and new wording, reason, evidence). Don't edit the rules file.
