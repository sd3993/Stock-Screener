# S4: pattern back-test (script, then an Opus medium helper for the mechanisms)

Inputs: `sample.csv`, `business_types.csv`, `outcomes.csv`, each sample company's `scr/` statements and `history.json`, the hypotheses H1-Hn in `experts.md`, and every testable general rule in `process/investing_rules.md`. Apply the tests in `README.md`, "What a pattern must pass".

1. **Features** (`features.csv` and `features_coverage.md`, one row per company and window start: `python -I tools/scripts/mb_features.py`, re-run on the final `sample.csv`; `--rows FILE` for any (sym, as_of) list, `--market --chart-only` for step 3; filing lags, basis choice and each feature's definition are in the script's header and code; years before screener's statements come from S3's parsed annual reports (`ar_statements.py`, column `src_ar`), kept apart from screener years in margin trends): only what was published before the start date (results from their filing date, else quarter end + 45 days, + 60 for Q4; price from the chart series on that date; the chart's TTM EPS and sales count from their filing too, since screener stamps some years' changes on the quarter end, so P/E is price ÷ that EPS). At least: market cap and band; TTM EPS and sales growth over 1 and 3 years and their acceleration; ROCE and ROE; operating margin and its trend; cash conversion (CFO ÷ net profit, 2 and 5 years); debt to equity and interest cover; P/E, P/E ÷ own 5-year median, PEG; dividend payout; share-count change (dilution); promoter holding, its change, pledge (filed from Mar 2009) and institutional holding, from screener/NSE's recent quarters and BSE's old patterns (`bse_shp.py`, S3) before them (`src_shp_via`); prior 1-year price return; business type and its attributes. Flag features that rely on a restated year.
2. **Score each hypothesis and rule**, per horizon:
   - frequency among winners, frequency among controls, lift, with counts;
   - for the whole sample and per business type (types with at least ~15 winners; smaller types reported as "too few");
   - in the discovery set (starts 2006-2015) and, unchanged, in the test set (2016-2023), and by regime and size band;
   - at the chosen threshold and at nearby thresholds;
   - for current rules: winners each rule would have rejected (missed) and controls it would have passed (false alarms).
3. **Whole-market check** for every pattern that passes 2: apply it at each past quarter-end start to every listed company in `outcomes.csv` with the features computable from the chart series (growth, P/E, P/E ÷ median, size, prior return); report how many it passed, the share that met the winner threshold against the base rate, and their median and mean multiples against all companies. For a business-type pattern, tag the business type (as in `s2_sample.md`) of the companies the pattern passes, only as many as needed, and score precision within that type.
4. **Mechanism check (helper, Opus medium):** given the script's surviving patterns and `experts.md`, for each surviving pattern, write the economic reason it should work and whether S5 or `experts.md` supports it; mark the rest "possibly chance".
5. Write `backtest.md`: per horizon, general patterns, business-type patterns, exit/avoid patterns, rejected patterns (and why), with every figure and sample size. Pick the S5 companies from here: winners the current rules caught and missed, and fallen winners, spread across business types and periods.
