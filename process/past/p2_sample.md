# P2.0: sample (script, run by the orchestrator, once per tagging round); the business types that P3.0 tags

From `outcomes.csv` (P1), pick winners and fallen winners, each with its reason, into `runs/past/<date>/sample.csv` (sym, group, horizon, window start, start market cap, sector, reason, matched_to). The comparison groups don't need picking here: they are the same-date market and same-type peers (`p8_features.md`).

**Groups, per horizon** (only companies with a start market cap of ₹50 cr or more):
- **Winners:** short = price 2x+ in 2 years; medium = 3x+ in 5 years (about 25% CAGR, the hurdle), with 5x+ as the strong group; long = 10x+ in 7-10 years. The per-share price multiple must roughly match the market-cap multiple; where it doesn't, the gap is dilution (or buybacks) and is noted.
- **Matched controls (frozen, 6 Nov 2026):** the first run paired 140 winners with a look-alike that didn't multiply (same start date and business type, nearest on growth, P/E and market cap; industry first, size widened step by step). The user replaced pair-by-pair matching with the comparisons in `p8_features.md` on 6 Nov 2026, so no more pairs are built or tagged, in this run or later ones. The 140 stay in `sample.csv` as a cross-check; each keeps its `size_step`, so it can be re-run on close-size pairs only. A winner without one simply has none.
- **Fallen winners:** reached the winner threshold inside the window, then gave back more than 50% from the peak (`pxpeak_*` against the end multiple). They test the exit rules.
- **Delisted and merged** names wherever their history covers the window.

**Business type (tagged in P3: helpers, Opus medium, batches of ~25):** classify each company by what it actually does and what drives its results, the same way F5 does (`process/future/f5_sector_map.md` step 1): screener's "about" text and industry label (`scr/overview.json`: `about` and `sectors`, screener's copy of NSE's classification) first; the segment split and business description in an annual report or presentation (from the exchange links in `scr/documents.json`) only when the about text leaves the type unclear, the company has several segments, or the business may have changed since the window start (user's choice, 3 Nov 2026, to save usage). The industry label is a coarse hint, never the answer: it is today's classification (wrong after a demerger or a change of business) and catch-all bins such as "Industrial Products" say little, so the about text or the window-start annual report decides; each batch file records whether it helped or misled (column `peers`, kept under that name: helped/misled/neutral). Screener's peers table isn't read: it is just the same industry's companies nearest in market cap, and it misled on about 15% of rows in the first six batches (3 Nov 2026). Provider sector labels are only a starting hint. Write `runs/past/<date>/business_types.csv` (sym, as_of, business_type, sub_type, revenue drivers, cyclicality: low/medium/high, capital intensity: low/medium/high, pricing power: low/medium/high, customers: B2C/B2B/government, evidence). Keep the type list short and economic, e.g. branded consumer, specialty manufacturer (engineered/B2B products), contract manufacturer/CDMO, capital goods and order-book businesses, EPC/infrastructure contractor, commodity producer or processor, lender, insurer/AMC/broker/platform, IT and knowledge services, healthcare services, pharma (generic/specialty/API), retail and distribution, real-estate developer, regulated utility, conglomerate/holding company; add a type only when companies don't fit (in `business_types/new_types*.md`); the list with its definitions goes to `business_types_list.md` beside the CSV (see Script). A company whose mix changed (e.g. commodity to specialty) is typed as of each window start. Tag winners and fallen winners here; P8 sends more companies to P3 only as needed. A company's tag applies at its other window starts too (`mb_features.py` marks this `bt_as_of`), unless its mix changed.

**Order (fewer tags, nothing lost to a usage cut-off):**
- Industry labels for the whole universe (`overview` only, every universe company without `scr/overview.json`); P8 draws peers from them.
- Fetch once for P2, P3 and P4: one background run, winners and fallen winners (`scr_company.py --syms-file <list> --only statements,quarterly,shareholding,peers,overview,documents,announcements,nse --skip-saved`), so P4 needs no second pass. Tagging helpers start on the first batches of 25 while it runs.
- Tag until `needs_next.csv` is empty; it lists only untagged winners and fallen winners.
- Each batch's input is built by `python tools/scripts/mb_tag_batch.py runs/past/<date>` (next 25 untagged, fetched companies from `needs_next.csv`); helpers follow `business_types/brief.md` in the run folder (a copy of `process/past/p3_tagging.md`). Each helper writes its own batch file (`business_types/batch_<n>.csv`); the orchestrator merges them into `business_types.csv`. A helper cut off mid-batch loses only that batch, which is re-run.

The selection itself is `tools/scripts/mb_sample.py` (see Script).

**Spread:** window starts across 2006-2023 so the 2008 crash, the 2011-13 slump, the 2018-19 mid-cap bear market, the 2020 crash and the 2023-24 boom all appear, with enough in 2006-2015 (discovery set) and 2016-2023 (test set) for each horizon. A company counts once per horizon (its first qualifying window) so long runs don't crowd the sample.

**Script:** `python -I tools/scripts/mb_sample.py [--seed 1]` writes `sample_candidates.csv` (winners, fallen winners and ~5 look-alike candidates per winner, each with its reason; a later run can ignore the candidates), seeded so the picks are reproducible, and prints counts per group, horizon, period and size band. `--repair <old sample_candidates.csv>` (after outcomes.csv changes, e.g. a fix) keeps each old winner and fallen winner whose first qualifying window and size band are unchanged, replaces the rest from the same stratum (group, horizon, period, size band; seeded; never a company already picked), recomputes every winner's candidates, and writes `sample_repair.md` (kept, replaced and why, winners with sector-level or fewer than 5 candidates, candidate lists changed) and `fetch_order.txt` (companies without the P2/P4 fetch, i.e. no `scr/documents.json`: new picks first, then candidates by rank); tags stay valid, as they are per (sym, as_of). `--candidates-only` belongs to the frozen matching and isn't used. Each candidate row's `match` says how it was drawn: industry, sector-level, type (no label, same tag) or type-industry (winner without a label). `--coverage` reports, writing nothing, how many winners in `sample_candidates.csv` would get 5 same-industry candidates, 5 with sector-level fill, fewer or none, with the labels saved so far. `python -I tools/scripts/mb_sample.py --finalize` (after each tagging round; `--companies <dir>` only for tests; `--dir DIR` reads and writes DIR instead of the run folder, for tests) does the checks below and writes `business_types.csv` (all `batch_<n>.csv` merged, with a `batch` column), `business_types_list.md` (the type list with definitions, from this file and `new_types*.md`), `sample.csv` (every passing winner, with its control where one is already tagged, those controls, passing fallen winners, each with its type and any warnings), `needs_next.csv` (the next round's tagging input: untagged winners and fallen winners; a flagged or artefact one asks for nothing) and `sample_checks.md` (counts, peers table, balance table, flagged rows, artefacts, warnings, malformed tag values, types not in the list, conflicting tags, and each winner's control status). An artefact needs evidence, since screener's prices are adjusted for splits and bonuses: a weekly price step of 1.5x or more that the market-cap-to-sales ratio contradicts beyond its one-decimal rounding, or that sits on a split/bonus ex-date (saved NSE corporate actions), a one-week spike that sets a fallen winner's peak, a step into the window end, a gap over 90 days hiding such a step, a stale start price, or a start market cap under ₹100 cr with a median weekly traded value under ₹5 lakh or 4+ of the 13 weeks before the start without volume. Steps it can't verify and a price multiple over twice the market-cap multiple are only warnings (the market cap from screener's ratios can be off). A candidate that is flagged or fails a check is skipped as a control.

**Checks (script, no helper):** `mb_sample.py` merges the batch files, counts the peers flags, and checks: groups, periods and size bands balanced; no data artefacts posing as winners (a price jump with no matching market-cap change, i.e. an unadjusted split or bonus; a start market cap near the ₹50 cr floor with thin trading; a series break). Shell companies or ones with no real operations are flagged by the tagging helpers, who read the about text anyway. The orchestrator drops or replaces flagged rows, re-runs the checks and records the final counts in `status.md`.
