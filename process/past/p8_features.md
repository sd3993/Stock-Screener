# P8.0: features and scoring (scripts, steps 1-8, in rounds); P9: mechanisms (Opus medium helper, steps 9-10)

Inputs: `sample.csv`, `business_types.csv`, `outcomes.csv`, each company's `scr/` statements and `history.json`, the hypotheses H1-Hn in `experts.md`, and every testable general rule in `process/investing_rules.md`. Apply the tests in `README.md`, "What a pattern must pass". Each round narrows the patterns and fetches only the data the survivors need; no model reads anything until P9. `patterns.csv` has one row per pattern and horizon, with its result at each stage or the stage it failed at, so a resumed round skips finished stages. A hypothesis or current rule that fails is kept in the results with its reason.

**P8.1: scan and market test (no fetching beyond what is saved)**

1. **Features** (`python -I tools/scripts/mb_features.py`: `features.csv` and `features_coverage.md`, one row per company and window start; `--rows FILE` for any (sym, as_of) list; `--market --chart-only --out market_features.csv` for every company at each quarter-end; `--market` without `--chart-only` once market statements are saved; filing lags, basis choice and each feature's definition are in the script's header and code; years before screener's statements come from P6's parsed annual reports (`ar_statements.py`, column `src_ar`), kept apart from screener years in margin trends): only what was published before the start date (results from their filing date, else quarter end + 45 days, + 60 for Q4; price from the chart series on that date; the chart's TTM EPS and sales count from their filing too, since screener stamps some years' changes on the quarter end, so P/E is price ÷ that EPS). At least: market cap and band; TTM EPS and sales growth over 1 and 3 years and their acceleration; ROCE and ROE; operating margin and its trend; cash conversion (CFO ÷ net profit, 2 and 5 years); debt to equity and interest cover; P/E, P/E ÷ own 5-year median, PEG; dividend payout; share-count change (dilution); promoter holding, its change, pledge (filed from Mar 2009) and institutional holding, from screener/NSE's recent quarters and BSE's old patterns (`bse_shp.py`, P5) before them (`src_shp_via`); prior 1-year price return; business type and its attributes. Flag features that rely on a restated year.
2. **Scan the winners, discovery set only** (`mb_patterns.py`, starts 2006-2015, per horizon, overall and per business type with at least ~15 winners; smaller types are reported as "too few"): every feature above, in a fixed list set before the scan, as its top and bottom same-date third and as the thresholds in `experts.md` and the current rules. For chart features the winners are every company in `outcomes.csv` that meets the horizon's threshold in the set and passes `mb_sample.py`'s artefact checks, each counted once per horizon and feature at its first qualifying window where that feature is known, chosen without looking at its value (known and total counts reported; business-type scopes use the tagged ones; test-set stages follow the same rule on 2016-2023). Statement and shareholding features stay on `sample.csv`'s winners and the panel. Keep what reaches frequency ≥ 50%. A script does this: no model reads winner stories first, since that produces convincing narratives.
3. **Market test, chart features:** each kept pattern against all companies below the winner threshold on the winner's own start date and in its size band; each date and band weigh by their number of winners. Lift ≥ 1.5 passes. Patterns using statements or shareholding can't be measured on the market before screener's years and BSE's coverage: they skip to step 4, marked, and face the market in step 6.

**P8.2: peers (P3-P7 fetch only what this needs)**

4. **Same-type peers:** `mb_patterns.py` writes `peer_panel.csv`: for each discovery-set winner, up to 3 companies of its business type on its start date, drawn at random (seeded) across all size bands, below the winner threshold, never picked for looking similar. It draws from companies already tagged, then from the same industry label (`scr/overview.json`) with P3 tagging only what the draw needs (`panel` lists those in `peer_untagged.csv`; re-run it after tagging). Fetch only the features surviving patterns use: statements (P4), annual reports (P6, P7; only those fields go to the fallback helpers), BSE shareholding (P5). Score lift ≥ 1.5 against the panel for fundamentals and against all same-type companies for chart features, and report it by size band. A pattern that fails overall but passes within a type stays a business-type candidate (`README.md`, test 6). Annual-report commands for the panel: `p4_statements.md`, "P6.0".
5. **Matched-pair cross-check:** survivors are also scored on the 140 frozen pairs (`p2_sample.md`), and on close-size pairs only; a big disagreement with step 4 is reported and looked into, not averaged away.

**P8.3: out of sample and whole market (statements from `market_fetch.txt`, fetched early by P4)**

6. **Out of sample, unchanged:** survivors on 2016-2023 windows, against the market and same-type peers, by regime and size band, at the chosen threshold and at nearby ones. Features with a long lookback start later than 2016 where screener's years run out; the coverage is stated with each figure.
7. **Whole-market precision:** each survivor at each past quarter-end start on every listed company in `outcomes.csv` with its features computable: how many it passed, the share that met the winner threshold against the base rate, and their median and mean multiples against all companies. For a business-type pattern, tag the business type (P3) of the companies the pattern passes, only as many as needed, and score precision within that type. For current rules: winners each rule would have rejected (missed) and companies it would have passed that didn't win (false alarms).
8. **Exit and avoid signals:** fallen winners against winners that held up, at each quarter-end after the company first reached the winner threshold, on discovery windows then test windows; then, for each signal, how often it was followed by a fall of more than 50% across the market, against how often that happens anyway. Nothing that needs knowing the peak.

**What runs unattended once `panel` is done** (first used 6 Nov 2026; each line one background task, its steps chained, with done and failed markers; files in the run folder):
- bseindia: `bse_shp.py --rows peer_panel.csv --gap 3`; `ar_statements.py plan --rows peer_panel.csv --groups peer`, `fetch`, `queue`; `mb_features.py --rows peer_panel.csv --out features_panel.csv`.
- screener: `mb_patterns.py market_fetch --force`; the fetch in `p4_statements.md`, "P4.0"; `mb_features.py --market --out market_features_full.csv`.
- local: `mb_patterns.py exits --write-rows`; `mb_features.py --rows exit_rows.csv --out features_exit.csv`; then a waiting task on the screener line's marker: `mb_patterns.py exits --exit-features features_exit.csv --market-features market_features_full.csv`.

By hand after these: P7 for the surviving fields, `features_panel.csv` again, `mb_patterns.py peers --panel-features features_panel.csv`, then `test`.

**P9**

9. **Mechanism check (P9 helper, Opus medium):** given the script's surviving patterns and `experts.md`, for each surviving pattern, write the economic reason it should work and whether P10 or `experts.md` supports it; mark the rest "possibly chance".
10. Write `backtest.md`: per horizon, general patterns, business-type patterns, exit/avoid patterns, rejected patterns (and why), with every figure, sample size and the number of patterns tried. Pick the P10 companies from here: winners the current rules caught and missed, and fallen winners, spread across business types and periods.
