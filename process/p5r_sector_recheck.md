# P5R: sector map recheck checklist (Opus medium, with `process/recheck.md`)

Work files: `<run>/sector_map.md` and `<run>/sector_of.tsv` (or the previous recheck's versions); your output: `sector_map_rc<N>.md` and `sector_of_rc<N>.tsv`. Read `process/p5_sector_map.md` first.

1. **Classification:** each company's sector and sub-sector against what it actually does (annual report or presentation business description, Tapetide profile text).
2. **Sources:** each tailwind and headwind has an official or named-expert source with a date; anything unsourced is marked and kept out of the tier.
3. **Contrary evidence** for each tier: headwinds the map missed for tier-1 sectors (policy reversals, overcapacity, import competition, price cycles) and tailwinds it missed for tier-3 sectors.
4. **Tiers:** each tier follows from the sourced evidence per the phase file's definitions.
